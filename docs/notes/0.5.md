# Neon 0.5

**She stops going quiet on you.** Some mornings she would wake to her name,
listen while you talked, and then drift off without ever answering — looking
for all the world like she hadn't heard a word.

She had. The model was taking the question, transcribing it correctly, and
then simply never replying: no answer, no error, nothing to wait for. Neon
made it invisible by treating that silence as an empty room, and hung up on a
question she had already understood.

Now an unanswered question gets asked again, in her own words, and if that
goes nowhere too she ends the session cleanly so the next "Hey Neon" starts
fresh. She also waits out a slow answer instead of dozing off on one —
replies can take longer than they feel like, and some were being cut off for
being merely late.

**Emotes keep their glow.** A smile or a raised brow reshapes her eyes by
cutting into them, and the cut was slicing the halo off along with it — the
light stopped dead on the flat edge, so a grin read as the eye being eaten
rather than shaped. The glow now follows whatever shape the expression
leaves.
